# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-702
## REFINERY UNIT HIGH-PRESSURE VALVES & HYDROSTATIC TESTING PROTOCOLS

### 1.0 PURPOSE & SCOPE
This standard operating procedure governs the periodic inspection, maintenance, and hydro-testing of high-pressure seal valves (VLV-4B series) installed across Refinery Unit 4B and allied processing loops.

### 2.0 HYDROSTATIC TESTING SPECIFICATIONS
| Parameter | Permissible Threshold | Test Duration | Action upon Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test Pressure | 150 PSI minimum | 45 minutes continuous | Immediate depressurization & seal check |
| Permissible Leakage | 0.00 mL/min (Zero tolerance) | Full test duration | Gasket replacement & re-test |
| Torque Rating | 120 Nm ± 5 Nm | Pre-test inspection | Re-torque flange bolts in star pattern |

### 3.0 MANDATORY SIGN-OFF REQUIREMENTS
Every hydrostatic test must produce an official Approval Note containing:
1. Plant Unit Identification (e.g., Refinery Unit 4B)
2. Equipment Tag Number (e.g., VLV-4B-03)
3. Inspector name, date, and hydrostatic pressure reading
4. Explicit confirmation of zero leakage and gasket replacement status
5. Recommendation for commissioning with authorization validity up to 12 calendar months.

### 4.0 SUPERSEDED SPECIFICATIONS
Notice: Revision 3 supersedes Revision 2. Pressure seal valves must not be operated at temperatures exceeding 350°C without secondary cooling jackets.
